Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 | Application: Elysium Vanguard AI OS (com.elysium369.meet)

This Privacy Policy describes how Elysium Vanguard collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal and diagnostic data when you use the Android application Elysium Vanguard AI OS and related services.

1. Principles & Data Minimization

We operate under a strict "Local-First and Privacy-Preserving" architecture:

  • Automotive Diagnostic Data: Real-time ECU telemetry, DTC trouble codes, and sensor readings from your ELM327/OBD2 adapter are processed and rendered entirely on your device.
  • No Unauthenticated Transmission: Diagnostic logs and 3D digital twins are stored locally on-device unless you explicitly choose to authenticate and sync certified reports.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data, location data, or vehicle inspection records.

2. Data We Collect and Why

A. Automotive & Diagnostics (Optional & User-Initiated)

  • Data Type: Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), live sensor PIDs (RPM, speed, coolant temp, fuel trims), VIN (when supported by vehicle ECU), and battery voltage.
  • Purpose: To diagnose vehicle failures, display real-time dashboard gauges, verify component repairs, and generate certified forensic PDF reports with cryptographic SHA-256 integrity hashes.
  • Storage: Local device SQLite/Room database. Syncs to encrypted cloud database (Supabase) only upon user request.

B. Location Services (Foreground Only)

  • Data Type: Precise (GPS) and approximate location.
  • Purpose:
    1. Required by Android OS to perform Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and classic Bluetooth discovery of OBD2 ELM327 scanners.
    2. Real-time navigation, road intelligence, speed calculation, and optional mobility dispatch operations while the driver is actively engaged in a trip.
  • Foreground Service: Location tracking runs only as an active Android Foreground Service with an ongoing user notification. It never operates silently in the background without user initiation.

C. Account & Authentication Data (Optional)

  • Data Type: Email address and authentication tokens via Supabase Auth.
  • Purpose: Cloud backup, multi-device sync of workshop inspection history, and workshop profile management.

3. Permissions Requested & Justification

  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT & BLUETOOTH_SCAN: To connect and communicate with OBD2 Bluetooth adapters.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: For Bluetooth scanning and active trip route telemetry.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE: To maintain stable, uninterrupted serial communication with the vehicle ECU during live diagnostic sessions.
  • CAMERA: Optional; used strictly for scanning VIN barcodes and QR codes on inspection certificates. No facial recognition templates are ever stored.
  • NFC: Optional; for digital vehicle key and access features using Host Card Emulation (HCE).

4. Data Retention & Security

All network communication is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS and TLS protocols. Cleartext HTTP traffic is explicitly prohibited across the entire application by Android Network Security Configuration.

Personal and diagnostic data are retained only as long as you maintain an active account or until you request deletion.

5. Account Deletion & Right to Be Forgotten

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy and international privacy standards, you have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Settings → Account Section → Tap "ELIMINAR CUENTA". Confirming this dialog submits a server-side account deletion request, immediately wipes all locally stored cache, revokes provisioning credentials, and signs you out.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: You can submit a permanent server-side data erasure request at any time without having the app installed by visiting our deletion portal: https://elysium-vanguard.app/delete-account.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services: Core platform APIs, Play Billing, and barcode scanning models.
  • MapLibre GL / OpenStreetMap: Privacy-respecting vector map rendering without personalized tracking.
  • Supabase: Encrypted backend database with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
